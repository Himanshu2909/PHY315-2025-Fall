## Abstract 1
We propose to synthesize two novel layered tellurides, **NbNi₂Te₂** and **NbNi₁.₂Fe₀.₈Te₂**, via the chemical vapor transport (CVT) method and to characterize their composition, structure, and electronic properties. In CVT, elemental precursors (Nb, Ni, Fe, Te) are sealed with a halogen transport agent in a temperature-gradient furnace, yielding single crystals at the cold end [(1)](https://taufourlab.faculty.ucdavis.edu/vapor-transport/#:~:text=1,1). The resulting van der Waals crystals will be analyzed by EDX to verify the Ni/Fe/Te stoichiometry [(2)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy-dispersive_X-ray_spectroscopy#:~:text=Energy,than%20experimental%20resolution%20of%20a) and by X-ray diffraction to determine the crystal structure (lattice parameters and space group). Based on related compounds (e.g. NbFeTe₂), we expect a layered orthorhombic/monoclinic structure [(3)](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2024/cc/d4cc01160k#:~:text=A%20van%20der%20Waals%20telluride%2C,necessary%20prerequisites%20for%20needed%20spintronics)[journals.aps.org](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.100.144102#:~:text=The%20high,primarily%20due%20to%20steric%20effects). The temperature-dependent electrical resistance R(T) will be measured to probe metallic versus insulating behavior and to detect any phase transitions (magnetic or superconducting) [(4)](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2024/cc/d4cc01160k#:~:text=A%20van%20der%20Waals%20telluride%2C,necessary%20prerequisites%20for%20needed%20spintronics) [(5)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_resistivity_and_conductivity#:~:text=the%20lattice%20is%2C%20the%20less,14). For example, NbFeTe₂ is metallic and ferromagnetic below ~87 K [(6)](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2024/cc/d4cc01160k#:~:text=A%20van%20der%20Waals%20telluride%2C,necessary%20prerequisites%20for%20needed%20spintronics), so the Fe-doped NbNiTe₂ may similarly exhibit magnetic ordering. In R(T) we will interpret metallic (positive dR/dT) behavior vs anomalies (kinks at a Curie or CDW temperature, zero resistance for superconductivity). These results will be interpreted in the context of known layered tellurides and topological/ magnetic materials [(7)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13596#:~:text=behaviors%20in%20crystal%20symmetry%2C%20electronic,Neutron%20diffraction) [(8)](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2506.13908#:~:text=,the%20evolution%20of%20Wannier%20charge). This study will not only establish the synthesis routes and basic properties of NbNi₂Te₂ and NbNi₁.₂Fe₀.₈Te₂, but also explore their potential as novel van der Waals materials for electronic or spintronic applications.

Layered ternary transition-metal tellurides represent a frontier in materials science, offering a rich platform for the discovery of novel electronic and magnetic phenomena, including topological states, complex magnetism, and superconductivity. This project reports the first successful synthesis of the novel van der Waals (vdW) material $NbNi_2Te_2$ and its iron-doped variant, $NbNi_{1.2}Fe_{0.8}Te_2$. High-quality single crystals were grown via the Chemical Vapor Transport (CVT) method using iodine as a transport agent.
